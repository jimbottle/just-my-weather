Just My Weather — Android permissions explained
================================================

The rationale for each permission in the MERGED manifest (what Play
Console sees after library manifests are merged in — check with
`aapt2 dump permissions app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`).
None of these is a Play "sensitive permission", so there is no
justification form to fill in. The Data safety form and the privacy
policy still describe them; keep the three in sync.

Declared in app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
--------------------------------------------

INTERNET
  Fetch observations and forecasts from the US National Weather Service
  (api.weather.gov) and, for daily forecasts past seven days, Open-Meteo
  (api.open-meteo.com). The only payload is the coordinates of the place
  being viewed. No other hosts are contacted.

ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  Decide whether a fetch is worth attempting before making it, so an
  offline phone shows the cached reading instead of a spinner.

ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION  (runtime, optional)
  Resolve the NWS grid point for where the user is. Coarse only; the app
  never requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or background location. Declining
  it leaves the app fully usable with a saved place.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS  (runtime on Android 13+, optional)
  Deliver the user's own alert rules as local notifications. No
  notification is ever sent without a rule the user created.

Merged in by androidx.work (WorkManager), not declared by the app
----------------------------------------------------------------

WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE
  WorkManager's standard set so the periodic alert-poll survives a
  reboot and can finish a check the device would otherwise doze through.
  The app never starts a foreground service of its own and never shows
  a foreground-service notification.

<queries> (package visibility, not a permission)
  Names the Gadgetbridge package variants so the optional "Send to
  Gadgetbridge" hand-off can detect whether Gadgetbridge is installed.
  Visibility only; no data is read from it.
